Book 01 · The STUCK Saga

STUCK: A Gangsta's Matrix

“They should've never counted me out!”

Deamon Pearsey, a.k.a. Kruger, has just been released from a Georgia penitentiary with an appetite for success. Fueled by the cold shoulders he received while incarcerated he's more than determined to come up. With the aid of Gunslanga and their Summer Street family he jumps off the porch with a vengeance.

Read the full synopsis

From dealing with his parole officer and balancing out his numerous affairs, Kruger gracefully brings you into his world. From gunplay in broad day to pulling all-nighters, losing was far from optional.

On the other hand, Lt. Honeycutt and his band of agents are obsessed with taking down Kruger and his crew, and he's far more than confident that he'll do just that. But a certain turn of events will make the lieutenant's mission a little harder than he ever imagined.

Let Kruger tell it, “They should've never counted me out!”

Book 02 · The STUCK Saga

STILL STUCK: The Money Train

More money, more problems.

The saga continues as Deamon Pearsey, a.k.a. Kruger, transitions from a pharmacist to a distributor. His only regret is making this transition without Gunslanga, who is still on the run.

Read the full synopsis

For Kruger and his Summer Street comrades the drama never ceases. Between Lieutenant Honeycutt and the other different band of agents searching for Gunslanga on a daily basis, Summer Street has become a regular pit stop for the people with badges.

That old saying more money more problems is becoming more vivid to Kruger as he may be facing a hydra that defies any easy solution. The Money Train isn't just about Kruger and his crew chasing money, it's more about what money turns people into.

Book 03 · Urban Crime

Blind Loyalty

Loyalty isn't always honorable. Sometimes it's fatal.

Blind Loyalty is a thrilling urban crime novel about trust, control and the deadly consequences of standing on the wrong side.

Read the full synopsis

Melanie Garvey has reached that cornerstone where she wants more than just a companion. She's a woman that seems to have it all together, but reluctantly, she's her biggest critic. She's always questioning her position whether it's with her career or her personal life with Blessed.

Blessed, on the other hand, has not one question on where he wants to be, he's just more concentrated on surviving the storm he has found himself in the midst of.

In this world, loyalty isn't always honorable — sometimes it's fatal.
